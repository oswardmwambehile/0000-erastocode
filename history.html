Our History

Matema Lutheran Hospital has a long and rich history rooted in faith, service, and dedication to healthcare. The hospital was established as part of the apostolic mission of matema Lutheran to serve the people of Southern Tanzania, regardless of faith or social background.

From its humble beginnings, the hospital has grown into a trusted referral center, providing quality medical services to thousands of patients every year.

Early Years

  • 1906: The mission cross was planted at Ndanda.
  • 1908: Benedictine Sisters began caring for the sick.
  • 1910: Construction of a small hospital started.
  • 1916: Ndanda served as a military hospital during World War I.
  • 1927: A new hospital building was officially opened.

Growth & Development

Over the decades, the hospital expanded its services, infrastructure, and training programs. Nursing and medical training institutions were introduced to support the growing demand for healthcare professionals.

New wards, laboratories, operating theatres, and specialized services were added, transforming the hospital into a modern referral facility.

The Hospital Today

Today, St. Benedict Ndanda Hospital is a Regional Referral Hospital serving patients from Mtwara, Lindi, Ruvuma, and beyond — including Dar es Salaam, Zanzibar, and neighboring countries.

Guided by Christian values and professional excellence, the hospital continues its mission of healing, compassion, and service to humanity.
